# Payments & Payouts

## Affiliate payouts

Affiliate commissions normally flow according to the affiliate network's payout settings. Configure your own payout account directly inside the legitimate platform and verify the account there.

## Direct sales

For products you sell yourself, use an established payment processor and its supported checkout/integration. Keep order records, refund terms, customer support information, and accurate product descriptions.

## Security rules

* Never send gift cards or crypto to "verify" a payout account.
* Never give a stranger remote access or screen-share access to financial apps.
* Never put payment credentials in public code or GitBook pages.
* Verify webhook signatures/events before fulfilling an order.

## Important distinction

Connecting PayPal or another payout method determines **where legitimate earnings are paid**. It cannot create earnings by itself.
