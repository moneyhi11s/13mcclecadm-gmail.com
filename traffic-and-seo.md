# Traffic & SEO

## Organic traffic engine

Create content around real questions the target customer searches for. Prioritize buyer-intent comparisons, tutorials, problem-solving guides, FAQs, and demonstrations that naturally connect to the offer.

## Distribution

Repurpose each useful idea into formats appropriate for channels you actually control. Link people to a relevant page rather than spamming unrelated audiences.

## Paid traffic

Do not automate paid advertising until conversion tracking works. Start with a capped test budget, define the maximum acceptable cost per acquisition, and stop campaigns that exceed it.

## Avoid

Bots, purchased fake engagement, unsolicited bulk messages, misleading redirects, fake testimonials, and prohibited ad claims can destroy both affiliate and payment accounts.
