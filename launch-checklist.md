# Launch Checklist

## Required before launch

* A real product/service or approved affiliate offer
* Correct affiliate/checkout URL
* A landing page with a clear benefit and CTA
* Privacy policy, terms, refund information where applicable, and affiliate disclosure
* Analytics and conversion tracking
* Payment/payout account configured in the merchant or affiliate platform
* Test purchase/click path completed before promotion

## Automation-ready checklist

* Lead form sends contacts to an authorized email workflow
* Welcome/follow-up messages are scheduled
* Links contain consistent campaign tracking parameters
* Failed links and forms are checked regularly
* Revenue and ad spend are recorded
* Stop-loss rules exist for paid campaigns

{% hint style="success" %}
Automate repeatable work—not trust. Review offers, claims, refunds, chargebacks, and campaign profitability regularly.
{% endhint %}
