## Traffic plan

### Phase 1 — validation

Publish problem-solving and curiosity-driven content aimed at people already researching affiliate marketing, online business systems and beginner digital marketing. Every post should provide standalone value and use the tracked offer only when relevant.

Test these angles:

1. **Complexity angle:** overwhelmed by piecing together an online business.
2. **Beginner angle:** wants to understand how a packaged digital system works.
3. **Time angle:** wants a more structured starting point instead of researching dozens of tools.
4. **Comparison angle:** packaged system versus building a funnel manually.

### Phase 2 — optimization

Measure outbound clicks and sales separately by traffic source. Keep the strongest source/angle combination and replace weak creative rather than increasing volume blindly.

### Phase 3 — paid scaling

Only introduce paid traffic after attribution is working. Begin with a capped test budget. Never bid or advertise in ways prohibited by the seller, Explodely, or the advertising platform.

## Decision rules

* No clicks → fix hook/distribution.
* Clicks but no sales → fix audience/pre-sell alignment before buying more traffic.
* Sales but excessive refunds → stop scaling and reassess offer quality.
* Repeatable profitable conversions → increase traffic gradually.
* Account warning/ad rejection → stop the affected tactic and correct it; never evade enforcement.
