# Moneyhi11s Revenue Hub

A practical operating system for building legitimate online revenue. This hub focuses on actions that can be automated after the required accounts, offers, tracking, and payment details are configured.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Automation does not create guaranteed income. Never pay someone to "unlock" earnings, send gift cards/crypto for verification, fake traffic, or use misleading claims. Revenue requires real customers or valid affiliate conversions.
{% endhint %}

## Revenue engine

1. Choose a legitimate offer with clear demand and acceptable margins/commission.
2. Build a focused landing page around one customer problem.
3. Connect the offer using your approved affiliate or checkout link.
4. Capture leads with permission and follow up automatically.
5. Publish useful content that attracts the right audience.
6. Track clicks, leads, sales, conversion rate, refunds, and profit.
7. Keep winners and stop campaigns that lose money.

## Start here

Use the pages in this hub as the build order: **Launch Checklist → Offers → Explodely → Funnels → Traffic → Automation → Payments → Analytics.**
