# Analytics & Scaling

## Core numbers

Track sessions, outbound clicks, leads, sales, conversion rate, revenue, commissions, refunds, advertising cost, cost per acquisition, and net profit.

## Key formulas

**Conversion rate = sales ÷ qualified visitors × 100**

**Profit = revenue/commission − advertising − software − refunds/chargebacks − other variable costs**

**Return on ad spend = attributable revenue ÷ ad spend**

## Scaling rule

Do not scale because a campaign has clicks. Scale only after tracking shows repeatable profitable conversions over a meaningful sample.

## Weekly review

Keep: profitable sources and pages. Improve: high-intent pages with weak conversion. Stop: sources consistently exceeding your acquisition limit. Expand: topics and offers supported by actual conversion data.
