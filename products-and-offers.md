# Products & Offers

## Offer scorecard

Score every potential offer on: customer problem, proof of demand, commission or gross margin, refund rate, payout terms, permitted traffic sources, creative assets, competition, and customer value.

## Best operating principle

Start with **one audience + one problem + one primary offer**. A small focused funnel is easier to measure and improve than a store full of unrelated products.

## Digital products

Useful categories can include templates, guides, checklists, training, productivity resources, and tools—provided the product delivers what its marketing promises.

## Affiliate offers

Read the program's terms before promotion. Never self-refer, cookie-stuff, impersonate a brand, buy prohibited traffic, or make earnings/health claims the merchant does not authorize.
