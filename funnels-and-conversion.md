# Funnels & Conversion

## Simple affiliate funnel

**Useful content → landing page → CTA → merchant checkout → follow-up/content loop**

## Landing page structure

* Specific problem and outcome
* Who the offer is for
* Key benefits
* Evidence that is truthful and supportable
* Clear CTA
* Disclosure when using affiliate links
* FAQ addressing genuine objections

## Improve conversion

Test one meaningful variable at a time: headline, offer positioning, CTA, page length, lead magnet, or traffic source. Measure the resulting conversion rate rather than choosing based on appearance alone.
